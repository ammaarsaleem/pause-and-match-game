Its motion

Pivot moves the point a piece turns around. At 100% it sits on the edge of the piece; past that it sits well outside, so the piece sweeps a wide arc like a planet. A far pivot makes the piece travel much further per degree, so raise the catch tolerance to match. Applies to spin, swing, carousel and zoom.

Negative runs it backwards. Give pieces speeds with no common factor — 2 and 3, or 3 and 4 — and they meet on exactly one frame.
