Shadow style

True colour keeps the object's own colours. Flat replaces them with one high-contrast tone. Opacity applies to whole-object pieces; a piece cut out of an object is always solid so it merges with its body when it lands.

The rest of the object stays put and becomes the shadow the piece has to drop back into. The cyan outline shows what gets cut.

This piece looks the same at every angle, so a spin gives the viewer nothing to read. Switch it to Square, move it over an edge of the object, or give it Drift or Zoom instead.

Its motion

Negative runs it backwards. Give pieces speeds with no common factor — 2 and 3, or 3 and 4 — and they meet on exactly one frame.

Background

Scene motion

Sweep carries pieces off one edge and back on the other. Carousel swings every cut piece around their shared centre. Cycles stay whole numbers so the clip loops seamlessly.

Difficulty

The slot is the ghost showing where each piece belongs. Drop it to 0% and the target disappears — the viewer has to judge the fit from the scene alone.

Slot is as solid as the moving piece, so the two look identical when landed and the puzzle reads as already solved.
